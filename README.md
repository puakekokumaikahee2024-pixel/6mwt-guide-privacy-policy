# Privacy Policy for 6MWT Guide

**Effective Date:** May 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how 6MWT Guide ("the App") handles user information. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

The App is designed to operate **without collecting personally identifiable information**.

- All user inputs (such as age, weight, height, activity selections, and test results) are stored **locally on your device only**.
- We do **not** collect, transmit, or store your personal data on any external server.
- We do **not** use analytics tools or tracking technologies that identify individual users.

---

## 2. Use of Information

Information entered into the App is used solely for the following purposes on your device:

- Calculating estimated metabolic equivalents (METs) and VO₂ values based on your inputs.
- Providing reference information about physical activities.
- Saving your activity records locally for your personal review.

We do not access, view, or share this information.

---

## 3. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services for subscription management and payment processing:

- **Apple App Store (StoreKit)** — for iOS in-app purchases and subscription management.
- **Google Play Billing** — for Android in-app purchases and subscription management.
- **RevenueCat** — for managing subscription entitlements across platforms.

These services may process anonymous transaction data in accordance with their own privacy policies. The App itself does not transmit any personally identifiable information to these services.

For details, please refer to:
- Apple Privacy Policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/
- Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- RevenueCat Privacy Policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

---

## 4. Subscription

The App offers an optional paid subscription ("6MWT Guide Pro") with a free trial period.

- Subscriptions are managed by Apple App Store or Google Play.
- Payment is charged to your Apple ID or Google account.
- You can manage or cancel your subscription at any time through your account settings.
- We do not have access to your payment information.

---

## 5. Disclaimer

The App is intended for **educational and reference purposes only**.

- The App is **not a medical device** and does **not** provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.
- Information provided by the App should not be used as a substitute for professional medical consultation.
- Always consult a qualified healthcare provider before starting any physical activity program.
- The developer assumes no responsibility for any injury, damage, or loss arising from the use of this App.

---

## 6. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.
